Market and Competitive Analysis

Market Size

We see ScamShield as a B2B product. The customers would be institutions that send UPI payments for their users, like cooperative banks, small finance banks and smaller payment apps (TPAPs), and the warning screens would be given to them as an SDK. End users would not pay anything.

For the total market we took published estimates of the Indian fraud detection and prevention market. These vary a lot: MarketsandMarkets puts it at about US$ 990 million in 2026, growing 22.4 % a year mkt_mm, while IMARC says US$ 1.4 billion in 2024, growing about 20 % a year mkt_imarc. We used the lower one. For the serviceable market we worked from UPI volume: about 241.6 billion transactions in FY2025-26 upi_fy26. If each payment check cost 2 paise (our own assumption), checking every UPI payment would be worth about ₹483 crore a year. For what we could realistically get in five years, we assumed 1 % of UPI volume from 5 to 10 smaller banks and apps.

Regulation also helps here: RBI's 2025 authentication directions expect risk-based checks on top of two-factor authentication from April 2026 rbi_auth, and RBI's own MuleHunter.AI shows that banks are willing to use AI for fraud mulehunter, mulehunter_mou.

Existing Solutions

Banks already run fraud risk management platforms such as FICO Falcon, Clari5, SAS or Feedzai. These monitor transactions in real time, but they are expensive, aimed at large banks, and usually hold or decline a payment quietly without telling the payer why. MuleHunter.AI, built by RBI Innovation Hub and used by about two dozen banks, finds mule accounts on the receiving side mulehunter, mulehunter_mou. The Department of Telecommunications' Financial Fraud Risk Indicator rates mobile numbers by fraud risk, and some payment apps use it to block payments dot_fri. Sanchar Saathi and Chakshu let citizens report fraud calls, which has led to lakhs of numbers being disconnected sancharsaathi. The 1930 helpline and CFCFRMS help freeze money after a complaint cfcfrms_saved. The big UPI apps show some warnings of their own, but how these work is not public. Caller ID apps like Truecaller flag spam calls but never see the payment. Abroad, the UK has made banks reimburse APP fraud victims since October 2024, with the cost split between the sending and receiving banks uk_psr, which gives banks a strong reason to stop scams before payment.

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Rules and calibrated ML together, with clear reasons
  • Warnings and friction aimed at the payer
  • Fast (p95 about 110 ms) and keeps working if ML is down
  • Evaluation that anyone can rerun

Weaknesses

  • Only synthetic data so far
  • No real bank or NPCI integration
  • The questions depend on the user being honest
  • Small student team with no sales experience

Opportunities

  • RBI's push for risk-based checks rbi_auth
  • Market growing about 20 % a year
  • Many small banks with no data science team
  • Public signals like DoT FRI to add later

Threats

  • Big apps and vendors adding similar features
  • Scammers coaching victims through warnings
  • Cost of compliance and audits
  • False alarms losing user trust

Unique Value Proposition

Our pitch in one line is: stop the scam before the money moves. ScamShield does not just produce a score for the bank. It tells the payer in one sentence why a payment looks wrong, asks the questions an analyst would ask, and only slows them down when the risk is real. When an analyst confirms a scam account, every other user is protected from it. And because it is light and runs on modest hardware, smaller banks and apps can afford it.
