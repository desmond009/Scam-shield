Market and Competitive Analysis

Market Size and Opportunity

ScamShield is positioned as a business-to-business (B2B) risk engine for institutions that originate UPI payments — banks, small finance and cooperative banks, and third-party application providers (TPAPs) — with the consumer-facing intervention screens supplied as a software development kit (SDK). We estimate the market in two ways: top-down from published market studies, and bottom-up from UPI volumes. Every assumption is stated so that it can be challenged.

Top-down: fraud detection and prevention in India

Published estimates of the Indian fraud detection and prevention (FDP) market vary widely. MarketsandMarkets estimates about US$ 990 million in 2026, growing at 22.4 % a year to about US$ 2.7 billion by 2031 mkt_mm; IMARC estimates US$ 1.4 billion in 2024, growing at about 20 % a year to US$ 8.9 billion by 2033 mkt_imarc. The two studies use different scopes and base years, so we take the lower figure as a conservative TAM. Both agree on a growth rate of 20–22 % a year.

Bottom-up: pre-transaction scam scoring for UPI

UPI processed about 24,162 crore (≈ 241.6 billion) transactions in FY2025-26 upi_fy26. If every UPI payment initiated by a customer were scored by a pre-transaction scam engine priced at an assumed 2 paise per scored payment, the annual spend would be about 241.6 × 109 × ₹0.02 ≈ ₹483 crore. This is the serviceable market for the specific capability we offer.

SWOT Analysis

Strengths

  • Hybrid engine: explainable rules + calibrated ML, with hard overrides
  • Payer-facing, proportional intervention and context questions
  • Low latency (p95 ≈ 110 ms) and graceful degradation
  • Honest, reproducible evaluation harness and simulator
  • Runtime-tunable rules and thresholds with what-if preview

Weaknesses

  • Trained and evaluated only on synthetic data
  • No integration with NPCI or real bank systems yet
  • Questionnaire relies on the user's honesty
  • Single-instance deployment tested only to ≈ 20 requests/s
  • Student team without sales or compliance track record

Opportunities

  • RBI's 2025 risk-based authentication directions rbi_auth
  • Fast-growing FDP market (≈ 20 % a year) mkt_mm, mkt_imarc
  • Thousands of cooperative and small banks lacking in-house data science
  • Integration of public signals (DoT FRI, Suspect Registry)
  • Multilingual warnings for first-time digital users

Threats

  • Large UPI apps and established FRM vendors adding similar features
  • Scammers adapting scripts to coach victims through warnings
  • Regulatory and data-protection obligations raise the cost of entry
  • False positives damaging user trust and institution adoption
  • Long B2B sales and security-audit cycles in banking

Unique Value Proposition

For banks and payment apps whose customers are being talked into paying scammers, ScamShield is a pre-transaction scam-risk engine that stops the scam before the money moves — explaining the risk to the payer in one sentence, asking the questions a fraud analyst would ask, and adding friction only when it matters. Unlike conventional fraud-monitoring platforms that silently decline or hold payments, ScamShield turns the payer into the last line of defence, and turns every confirmed scam into protection for every other user.

Four things make the proposition distinctive:

  1. Intervention, not just detection. The output is a designed user experience (none / warn and ask / pause and verify), not only a score.
  2. Explainable by construction. Every decision carries reason codes rendered as plain sentences for users and technical detail for analysts.
  3. Measured honestly. The evaluation harness compares rules, ML and hybrid on a time-based holdout and publishes the trade-offs, including where the hybrid is worse.
  4. Built for smaller institutions. Container-based, runs on modest hardware, and lets operations staff tune rules without code changes.
