PRD Summary

Product requirements summary for a pilot release
ItemDetails
ProductPre-payment scam risk engine with warning screens for payers and consoles for analysts and admins
CustomersCooperative banks, small finance banks and smaller UPI apps
UsersPayers (the four personas), fraud analysts, risk admins
GoalsFewer successful scam payments, almost no friction on genuine ones, and faster blocking of scam accounts
Not in v1Blocking calls or SMS, card or net banking fraud, automatic blocking without an analyst (except confirmed scam payees)
Release criteriaIn shadow mode on partner data: precision of at least 50 % at HIGH and an FPR of at most 0.5 % at MEDIUM. Also p95 under 300 ms at pilot load, no critical security findings, and a consent flow approved by the partner
Open questionsWho is liable for a missed scam? How do blocked account owners appeal? Which languages come first?

Risks and Mitigation

Main risks
RiskLikelihood / impactWhat we would do
Works worse on real dataHigh / highShadow mode first, re-tune on real labels; the rules still give a baseline
Too many false alarmsMedium / highNothing shown for LOW, track FPR per user group, preview rule changes, switch off a rule quickly
Scammers coach victims past warningsHigh / mediumAnswers only raise risk, OTP override, payee signals that do not depend on the victim
Data breachLow / highCollect less, encrypt, control access, incident plan, host in India
ML service outageMedium / mediumTimeout, circuit breaker, rules-only mode (already built)
Big players copy the ideaHigh / mediumFocus on small institutions and work alongside existing FRM tools
Fake mass reportingMedium / lowOne report per account; only analysts can block
