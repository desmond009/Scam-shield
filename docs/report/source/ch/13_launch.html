Product Requirements Document (Summary)

PRD summary for ScamShield v1.0 (pilot release)
ItemContent
ProductScamShield — pre-transaction scam-risk engine with payer-facing interventions and analyst/admin consoles.
ProblemUsers are manipulated into authorising payments to scammers; existing controls verify identity, not intent, and act after the money has moved.
Target customersCooperative banks, small finance banks and smaller TPAPs originating UPI payments.
Target usersPayers (personas: senior citizen, student, shopkeeper, salaried); fraud analysts; risk administrators.
Goals(1) Reduce successful scam payments among intervened users; (2) keep friction on genuine payments negligible; (3) shorten time from first victim to payee block.
Non-goals (v1)Telecom-level blocking; card or net-banking fraud; credit decisions; automatic blocking without analyst review (except confirmed scam payees).
Key featuresHybrid score + band + reasons; proportional interventions; questionnaire; payee reputation and reports; live analyst console; rules editor with what-if; evaluation and retraining; simulator; health dashboard.
Success metricsSee Table 13.4 (KPIs).
Release criteriaShadow-mode precision ≥ 50 % at HIGH and FPR ≤ 0.5 % at MEDIUM on partner data; p95 latency < 300 ms at pilot load; zero critical findings in security audit; DPDP notice and consent flow approved by partner's compliance team.
DependenciesPartner data access and labels; integration with partner's UPI app back end; hosting in India.
Open questionsWho bears liability for a missed scam? How are blocked-payee appeals handled? Which languages first?

Risk Analysis and Mitigation

Risk register (likelihood and impact: L = low, M = medium, H = high)
RiskLIMitigation
Model performs worse on real data than on synthetic dataHHShadow-mode pilot before any intervention; recalibrate and re-tune thresholds on real labels; rules provide a floor.
High false-positive rate annoys usersMHLOW band sees nothing; monitor FPR by segment; what-if preview before rule changes; kill-switch per rule.
Scammers coach victims through warningsHMAnswers only raise risk; OTP override; vary warning designs; payee-side signals (mule, reports) independent of victim.
Data breach or misuseLHData minimisation; encryption; access control; audit; CERT-In / DPDP incident plan; India-only hosting.
ML service outageMMTimeout + circuit breaker + rules-only degraded mode (implemented and tested).
Regulatory changeMMTrack RBI / NPCI / MeitY notifications; configurable rules and thresholds; modular consent.
Competition from large apps / vendorsHMFocus on underserved small institutions; integrate as a complement to existing FRM.
Malicious mass reporting of a payeeMLOne report per account; reports never block alone; analyst verdict required.
Key-person dependency in a small teamMMDocumentation (architecture, API, data, demo script); tests; shared code ownership.
